Drop it in, press Play

Casual servers usually throw out custom files. Eternal packs your mods the way cueki's casual-pre-loader does, so hitsounds, skins, particles and HUDs make it into matchmaking.

  1. Drag a mod onto the window

    A VPK, a folder or a ZIP, RAR or 7z straight from your downloads all work. New mods from GameBanana can be imported from the Home tab, checked against GameBanana's own virus scan.

  2. Put them in order

    When two mods change the same file, the one higher up wins. Each mod tells you which files it wins and which it loses, so you're never guessing why a skin didn't show up.

  3. Press Play

    A moment after each change, Eternal applies your mods. It never touches the game folder while TF2 is running, and Play waits for a build to finish instead of starting the game on half-written files.

  4. Changed your mind?

    Restore vanilla puts the game back the way Steam installed it. Your mods stay in the library, ready for next time.

Settings in plain English

Every option says what it does, whether it costs you frames and what TF2 uses by default. Change what you care about and the rest stays with TF2.

Binds by pressing the key
Click, press the key, mouse button or scroll wheel, then type the command.
Profiles to start from
Competitive, Max Performance, Max Quality and Balanced, or your own. A profile can remember your mods too, and you can send it to a friend as a file.
Search by name or cvar
Type "fov" or "viewmodel_fov", either finds it.

Your autoexec stays yours

What you change goes into one marked block at the end of autoexec.cfg. Everything outside it is left exactly as you wrote it, and the old file is kept as autoexec.cfg.eternal-backup.

This is the block after setting a field of view, fast weapon switch, damage numbers, a frame cap and one voice bind:

// === ETERNAL LAUNCHER MANAGED BLOCK — do not edit between these markers ===
// Gameplay
fov_desired 90
hud_fastswitch 1

// Combat Feedback
hud_combattext 1

// Weapon View
viewmodel_fov 70

// Performance
fps_max 240

// Binds
bind "f" "voicemenu 0 0"
// === ETERNAL LAUNCHER MANAGED BLOCK END ===

What's your backpack worth?

Eternal reads your public TF2 inventory from Steam and prices it with backpack.tf data from pricedb.io, in keys and refined, the way trades are written.

Pick an item to see what sellers ask, what buyers offer and its Steam Market price in your currency. Sort by value, or filter by quality, class or what you can trade.

Eight themes, pick one

Click a theme and this page switches to it, screenshot included. In the launcher you can also pick any accent colour, put your own TF2 screenshots behind it and make the whole window bigger or smaller.

Questions

Do mods really load in Casual? Most visual ones do: particles and unusual effects, weapon and cosmetic skins, war paints, map textures, props and HUDs. Full player-model swaps are hit or miss, because the server often forces the original model back. The Mods tab warns you about models with a known problem before you launch.

Does it change the game itself? No. Eternal never touches the game's code or memory. It writes the packed mods into tf/custom and one marked block into autoexec.cfg, the same places any HUD or hitsound goes.

Can I still start TF2 from Steam? Yes. Copy the launch line from Config, Launch Options into TF2's launch options in Steam so the preloader's setup still runs when the game boots.

Where do my mods live? In the launcher's own folder, not in tf/custom. Restore vanilla removes what was put in the game and keeps your library.

Does it cost anything? No. It's free and open source under the MIT license, with no account to make.

Something broke? Open an issue on GitHub and attach your logs. They're in ~/.local/share/eternal-launcher/logs on Linux and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Eternal TF2 Launcher\logs on Windows.

Built on cueki's preloader

The part that gets mods into Casual is cueki's casual-pre-loader. Eternal ships it unchanged and builds the launcher around it, so the credit for that part goes to cueki and its contributors. If all you want is the preloader, use the original.

Thanks also to backpack.tf and pricedb.io for prices, GameBanana and its creators, Deadlock Mod Manager for ideas on recovery and conflicts, and Jeremy Tribby for the Barlow typeface.
